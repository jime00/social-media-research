Dataset & Methodology

Secondary student survey data sourced from Kaggle, variable classifications, and empirical data preparation.

Kaggle Data Source

Our empirical investigation relies on secondary data sourced from Kaggle covering student social media usage metrics:

📁 Student Social Media & Academic Performance Dataset

The research analysis is being developed around a comprehensive student survey dataset hosted on Kaggle. The dataset contains observations covering demographic traits, estimated daily screen time, preferred social media channels, sleep hours, stress indicators, and self-reported academic outcomes.

Explore Dataset on Kaggle ↗ Hosted by Kaggle Community • Open Academic Dataset

Variables Evaluated from Dataset

How the records in our Kaggle dataset map directly to our 8 core project variables:

📱 Behavioral & Platform Variables

  • Daily social media usage: Total self-reported hours per day on networking apps.
  • Social media platforms used: Breakdown of usage across key networks (examples include, but are not limited to, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Twitter, Discord, YouTube, and others):
    Instagram TikTok Facebook Twitter / X Discord YouTube ✨ & Others

👤 Demographic Traits

  • Age: Chronological age distribution of student respondents.
  • Gender: Demographic subgroup categorization for comparative analysis.

🌙 Sleep & Physical Health

  • Sleep problems: Indicators measuring sleep duration, bedtime delays, and sleep comfort.
  • Overall well-being: Self-rated scale evaluating energy, physical comfort, and general health.

📚 Academic & Mental Health

  • Academic performance: GPA bands, reported study focus, and distraction frequency.
  • Stress and pressure: Reported emotional tension, academic workload stress, and coping habits.
