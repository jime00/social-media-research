Hypothesis Evaluation Status

Current evaluation status for the two primary hypotheses formulated in the research proposal:

Hypothesis 1 Pending Data Analysis

H1: Students who spend more time on social media may be more likely to use it as a distraction from personal problems, which could interfere with their ability to focus on schoolwork and daily responsibilities.

Target Metrics: Daily screen time, distraction indicator variables, and academic focus metrics.
Hypothesis 2 Pending Data Analysis

H2: Students who are easily distracted by social media may experience negative effects on their academic performance and sleep.

Target Metrics: Reported sleep quality/hours, distraction score, and academic GPA performance.
