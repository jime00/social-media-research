Research Proposal

An overview of the central research aims, foundational context, and real-world significance.

Research Topic

The impact of social media usage on students.

Real-World Problem

Social media has become an increasingly common part of students' daily lives. This project will explore how social media usage is associated with students' well-being, health, sleep, and daily habits.

Why This Problem Matters

Understanding social media usage can help students, educators, and researchers better understand how social media may negatively or positively affect students' lives. This includes areas such as sleep, academic performance, mental health, physical health, and overall well-being.

Research Inquiries

Our central research question and five guiding sub-questions targeting specific dimensions of student life.

Primary Research Question

How is social media usage associated with students' mental and physical health and daily habits?

Hypotheses

Formulated predictive statements to be evaluated through quantitative and exploratory data analysis.

Hypothesis 1 Pending Data Analysis

H1: Students who spend more time on social media may be more likely to use it as a distraction from personal problems, which could interfere with their ability to focus on schoolwork and daily responsibilities.

Hypothesis 2 Pending Data Analysis

H2: Students who are easily distracted by social media may experience negative effects on their academic performance and sleep.

Dataset & Methodology

Our empirical investigation relies on secondary data sourced from Kaggle covering student social media usage metrics.

Data Source Information

The research analysis is being developed around a comprehensive student survey dataset hosted on Kaggle. The dataset contains observations covering demographic traits, estimated daily screen time, preferred social media channels, sleep hours, stress indicators, and self-reported academic outcomes.
