Research Proposal

An overview of the central research aims, foundational context, and real-world significance.

Research Topic

The impact of social media usage on students.

Real-World Problem

Social media has become an increasingly common part of students' daily lives. This project will explore how social media usage is associated with students' well-being, health, sleep, and daily habits.

Why This Problem Matters

Understanding social media usage can help students, educators, and researchers better understand how social media may negatively or positively affect students' lives. This includes areas such as sleep, academic performance, mental health, physical health, and overall well-being.

Core Research Question

The guiding inquiry driving our semester-long empirical study:

Primary Research Question

How is social media usage associated with students' mental and physical health and daily habits?
